The Agent
Open Intelligent Assistant

The Agent is your open-source AI assistant that manages your digital world — from blazing fast chat and 50+ languages supported, through advanced image generation and analysis, to real-time web search, currency tracking, and custom alerts.

Host it on your own server, customize it to your needs, and enjoy complete privacy with the power of personal AI.

Core Features

Open, yet Private

The code is fully open-source for transparency, but your data and conversations remain private and accessible only to you. Self-host for even greater control.

Language Intelligence

The assistant understands and responds in your preferred language with 50+ languages supported, making conversations natural and accessible for users worldwide.

AI Imaging

Create new images, edit existing images using text instructions, or restore old and broken images using advanced AI models. Bring your ideas to life with powerful tools.

Web Search & Media

Get up-to-date information from the web, including news, currency prices, and more. Send images, voice messages, and documents for processing.

Zero Lock-in

Switch between chat apps and AI platforms without losing your chat history or settings. Keep your experience consistent and seamless.

Monitoring & Alerts

Stay informed with real-time monitoring of news, markets, and topics you care about. Get proactive notifications so you never miss a beat.
